Offset FX gives you the Offset look without the manual work. Select a layer, run a script from the Scripts panel, adjust the sliders while the preview updates, and press OK.

## The seven effects

- **Riso Duotone**: two-ink risograph print with five ink presets, misregistration, ink grain and contrast. Pick your own inks with the colour pickers.
- **Halftone**: an editable live Halftone filter with a print-style blend. Ink dots, black & white, CMYK, newsprint lines or a circular screen.
- **Photocopy**: a gritty zine copy with toner noise and copier streaks. It reads your photo first and sets a starting darkness for you.
- **Glitch Slices**: shifted slices, colour split and scan lines. Hit Shuffle for a new variation.
- **RGB Split**: red/cyan, three-way RGB or magenta/green split at any angle.
- **Film Grain**: live grain and vignette filters you can double-click and tweak later.
- **Texture Overlay**: pick any texture file and lay it over your layer with Multiply, Screen, Overlay and more, clipped to the layer. Made for the Shapeshifters texture packs.

## Non-destructive

Pixel effects are added as a new layer above your photo. Halftone and Film Grain are added as live filters inside the layer. Hide, mask or delete them whenever you like.

## Install

1. Download `Offset FX.afscripts` and import it from the Scripts panel in Affinity.
2. Select an image or pixel layer.
3. Run any Offset FX script from the Scripts panel.
