Posted Paper FX takes your photo off the screen and onto paper: printed, copied, stamped, pasted on a wall or torn off. Each script lands as a new layer, so your original stays untouched underneath.

## The seven effects

- **90s Print**: a faded, warm magazine print on cream paper, with four-ink dots slightly out of register.
- **Broken Copier**: a black-and-white copy dragged mid-scan, with smears, glass streaks, lamp banding and toner noise.
- **Glossy Print**: a glossy photo print with punchy colour, sheen, reflections, a white border and rounded corners.
- **Overprint**: prints a logo, text or photo like real ink, with uneven coverage, spread and a second shifted hit. Best on a logo or text layer.
- **Postage Stamp**: a perforated stamp with a paper border, optional ageing and a generic postmark.
- **Poster Paste-up**: a wheat-pasted poster with wrinkles, glue bubbles, stains and torn-off patches.
- **Torn Paper**: tears the photo along one or more edges, with a white paper-fibre rim and a soft shadow.

## Non-destructive

Every effect is added as a new layer above your photo. Lower its opacity, change its blend mode, mask it or delete it whenever you like.

## Install

Double-click **Posted Paper FX.afscripts** and Affinity adds all seven scripts to your Scripts panel as one set. Prefer only a few? The single .afscript files are in the download too. Affinity asks once whether to trust scripts from outside the app.
