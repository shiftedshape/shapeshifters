A hero that behaves like Figma or Affinity. Hover a layer and it gets a selection frame with eight handles, a name tag and its size. Drag it, resize it from the handles, and snap guides line it up with the other layers.

## Every layer has its own effect

- **3D tilt**: the card leans toward the pointer with glare and a colour bevel
- **Fluid**: a logo or wordmark smears in a four-colour fluid trail
- **Liquid**: a grey photo ripples like water and reveals its colour
- **Cloth**: the picture hangs like fabric; brush it or grab it and pull
- **Bend**: paper that curls up in the direction you flick it
- **Loop**: brand marks, a GIF or a video that pauses on hover

## Details

- Board layout drifts opposite the cursor and reveals layers beyond the edges; layers drop in with a bounce
- Your heading, a logo with its own fluid hover, or any Framer frame in the middle, plus an optional spinning round button
- Backgrounds: ruled lines, columns, grid, dots or an artboard on a pasteboard
- WebGL only starts for layers in view; plain images where WebGL isn't available

## What's included

- Framer component with every setting in the properties panel
- JavaScript version for any website, no dependencies
