Images float around your headline. Hovering one selects it the way Figma or Framer would: a frame with eight handles, a name tag with its size, and snap guides that line up with the other layers.

## Details

- Layers can be dragged; they tilt and bend while they move
- A WebGL liquid effect makes them bulge and ripple under the pointer
- Your own headline and buttons sit in the middle
- On phones the layers stack into a neat grid under the headline
- Colours for the frame, handles, tag and guides are all yours to set

## What's included

- Framer component: add layers and connect your headline in the properties panel
- JavaScript version for any website, with no dependencies
- Example page and a playground
