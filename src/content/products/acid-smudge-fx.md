Acid Smudge FX turns any photo into the smeared, acid-coloured and moody looks you see on posters, album covers and feeds. Pick a script, move the sliders while the preview updates, and press OK.

## The seven effects

- **Acid Map**: maps the photo to an acid palette with grain and optional poster steps. Try the Colour blend to keep the original detail.
- **Acid Smudge**: the signature look: a horizontal motion smear in bands, mapped to acid colours, with grain.
- **Echo Ghost**: fading copies of the photo trailing to one side, like a subject caught moving. Keep the colours or go acid.
- **Gloom**: crushed shadows, a muted tinted palette, a light smear, grain and a vignette.
- **Neon Sun**: a two-colour neon grade with a glowing offset outline, rainbow prism streaks, glitch bars and grain.
- **Noise Smudge**: gloomy TV smudge with heavy grain, scan lines, rolling noise bands and a vignette.
- **Pixel Stretch**: one strip of the photo stretched out to the edge, like a slit-scan glitch. Pick where it starts and which way it runs.

## Non-destructive

Every effect is added as a new layer above your photo. Lower its opacity, change its blend mode, mask it or delete it whenever you like.

## Install

Double-click **Acid Smudge FX.afscripts** and Affinity adds all seven scripts to your Scripts panel as one set. Prefer only a few? The single .afscript files are in the download too. Affinity asks once whether to trust scripts from outside the app.
