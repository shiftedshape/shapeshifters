The logo effect from the middle of Selection Stage FX, as a component of its own. Move over the letters and they smear and split into a fluid trail in your colours, with a soft halo around the edges. Click for a burst. When the pointer leaves, it settles back into a crisp logo.

## Works on

- **Type**: write your headline or wordmark, pick a font, weight and tracking. It always fills the component's width and stays sharp at any size.
- **Logos**: an SVG, PNG or WebP with a transparent background. SVGs are drawn at the size they're shown, so they stay crisp.

## Settings

- Four trail colours, strength, distortion, brush size, halo and colour cycle
- Tint: turn it down for only the distortion, without colour
- Alignment and padding, so the trail has room around the logo
- Shows the plain logo where WebGL isn't available or motion is reduced

## What's included

- Framer component with every setting in the properties panel
- JavaScript version for any website, no dependencies
