Halftone & Spray mixes two things: big spray-paint strokes pushed through a coarse halftone screen, and soft two-colour fades that look like riso prints.

The strokes work as bold backgrounds or as a texture layer over type. The fades are calmer and make great backdrops for product shots and quotes.

## What you get

- 16 unique textures, 4500 × 3000 px JPG
- Duplicates removed, files numbered 01–16
- A short README with blend-mode tips for Affinity
