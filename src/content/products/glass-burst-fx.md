Glass Burst FX puts your photo behind real-looking patterned glass. Every lens, rib and tile bends its own piece of the photo, so it reads as glass instead of a filter. Cover the whole picture, or one side, the top, the bottom or a band with a clean straight glass edge.

## The seven effects

- **Dot Glass**: pressed glass with round lenses: dots, hex dots, random bubbles, rings or dimples.
- **Fluted Glass**: the reeded, ribbed glass look, with soft shading per rib, a rainbow fringe and optional frost.
- **Liquid Glass**: a thick, smooth glass panel like current app design: frosted inside, bent at its rounded rim, with a rainbow edge.
- **Refracted Glass**: thick glass circles, pills, bands or organic blobs that shift and zoom the view inside them.
- **Scratched Glass**: a see-through layer of hairline scratches, scuffs, wipe marks, dust and smudge haze. Stack it over the other glass effects.
- **Tile Glass**: glass blocks and faceted glass: rounded blocks, bevelled tiles, diamonds, pyramids, triangles or hexagons.
- **Wave Glass**: flowing glass reliefs: wavy ribs, zigzag, herringbone, a water ripple around a point you pick, or hammered glass.

## Non-destructive

Every effect is added as a new layer above your photo. Lower its opacity, change its blend mode, mask it or delete it whenever you like.

## Install

Double-click **Glass Burst FX.afscripts** and Affinity adds all seven scripts to your Scripts panel as one set. Prefer only a few? The single .afscript files are in the download too. Affinity asks once whether to trust scripts from outside the app.
