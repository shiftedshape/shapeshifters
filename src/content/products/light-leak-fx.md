Light Leak FX adds the light that old cameras let in. Nothing is pasted from a stock overlay: every leak, flare and glow is drawn fresh for your photo, as its own Screen layer, and Shuffle gives you a new one each time.

## The four effects

- **Halation**: a red-orange film glow that bleeds around the bright highlights, made from the photo’s own light. Also in the free Starter Pack.
- **Lens Flare**: sun flare, anamorphic streak, vintage lens ghosts, starburst or soft glow, placed where you want the light.
- **Light Leak**: edge burns, colour bands and corner flares, like light hitting the film.
- **Prism Leak**: rainbow streaks, arcs and shards, like light through a prism, with an optional warm edge burn.

## Non-destructive

Every effect is added as a new layer above your photo. Lower its opacity, change its blend mode, mask it or delete it whenever you like.

## Install

Double-click **Light Leak FX.afscripts** and Affinity adds all four scripts to your Scripts panel as one set. Prefer only a few? The single .afscript files are in the download too. Affinity asks once whether to trust scripts from outside the app.
