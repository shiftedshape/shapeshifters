An old computer screen that actually behaves like one. Scanlines, a curved glass tube, glow that bleeds past the edges and a picture that bends where the pointer moves. Click it and it glitches.

## What it can show

- A scrolling neon grid
- Your own text, such as "GAME OVER"
- Your own image or video

## Frames

Comes with two close-up monitor frames, beige and graphite, plus a TV. Pick "None" for just the glowing screen, or use a photo of your own.

## What's included

- Framer component: paste one file, set everything in the properties panel
- JavaScript version for any website, with no dependencies
- The monitor frames, an example page and a playground to try settings
